Juxtapose

Take a property from one thing. Give it to another. Fight your way down before the dreamer wakes.

How to play

Move

W A S D
Run (keep running to break into a sprint)
Space
Jump · again mid-air for a flip · near a wall to kick off
Shift
Dash (once in the air)
C / Ctrl
Slide while running · ground-pound in the air
Mouse
Look; the camera swings behind you

Parkour

Jump alongside a wall to wall-run. Jump straight into one to climb. Run at a ledge to mantle or vault. Land on a brass rail to grind. Beds are trampolines. Slide-jump to keep your speed.

Lucidity

Absurd combos, such as stacking properties, new pairings, or giving things to yourself, raise Lucidity. That makes every property stronger and the dream stranger. At 100 the dreamer wakes and your run ends.

Memories

Each night that goes deep or strange enough leaves a memory behind. Memories add new properties to the dream.
